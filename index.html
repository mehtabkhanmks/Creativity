Transaction Types

Six ways to turn an idea into value

Beyond a simple "buy" — a full spectrum of how creativity flows between people.

01
💎
BUY

Purchase Full Rights

Complete ownership transfers to the buyer. The creator sells all rights — permanently and cleanly.

Ownership Effect: Full transfer to buyer
02
📜
LICENSE

Pay to Use

Buyer gets usage rights under defined terms. Creator retains full ownership and can license to others.

Ownership Effect: Creator retains ownership
03
📈
INVEST

Fund Development

Investor funds the project in exchange for equity or revenue share. Creator keeps creative control.

Ownership Effect: Negotiated equity/revenue share
04
🤝
COLLABORATE

Join to Build

A team forms around the idea. Equity and credit are defined up-front through platform-generated agreements.

Ownership Effect: Equity/credit split defined
05
❤️
SUPPORT

Tip / Patronage

Pure support — Patreon-style. No rights transfer. Believers fund creators they want to see succeed.

Ownership Effect: No rights transfer
06
💬
CONTACT

Open a Conversation

Discovery and negotiation phase. No commitment. Just the start of a conversation that could become a deal.

Ownership Effect: Discovery/negotiation only

Legal & IP Framework

Built on trust.
Designed for protection.

We know that an idea alone isn't legally protectable — we built the platform around that reality, not around false promises.

📋

Clear Legal Education

We tell creators exactly what protection they have — copyright on expression, NDA-gated trade-secret, or patent-pending if they've filed. No overselling.

🛡️

NDA-Gated Previews

Full idea details only visible under NDA. Buyers see a teaser — and commit to an NDA before the full pitch is revealed.

⚖️

Auto-Generated Legal Templates

Platform-drafted NDA, licensing agreement, IP assignment, and equity-split templates. Always labelled as templates — not legal advice.

🔍

Plagiarism & Dispute Process

Duplicate detection on upload. Clear DMCA-style takedown and counter-notice process. Mediation before escalation.
